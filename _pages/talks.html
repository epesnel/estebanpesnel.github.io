SCALED: Surrogate-gradient for Codec-Aware Learning of Downsampling in ABR Streaming

📍 PCS 2025 - Picture Coding Symposium · Aachen, Germany · December 2025
We introduce a codec-aware learning framework enabling end-to-end training of neural downscalers through non-differentiable video codecs. By leveraging data-driven surrogate gradients derived from true compression noise, we align training objectives with deployment performance, achieving over 6.11% BD-Rate improvement compared to codec-agnostic approaches.
